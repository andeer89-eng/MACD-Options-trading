# Monopoly lens
Score: 4 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- **Niche technical combination**: Pairing Stan Weinstein Stage 1–4 filtering, Mansfield Relative Strength, CBOE GEX, and shadow exit-policy tracking (`X1`/`X2`/`X3`) with fail-closed NYSE session gates addresses serious systematic discipline gaps that casual retail chart apps ignore.
- **Shadow signal outcome ledger**: Logging every confirmed signal and comparing taken vs. skipped trades (`regime`, `guard`, `size`) creates the seed of an auditable behavioral dataset if aggregated cleanly over time.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Zero 10x proprietary technology or moat against copycats**: MACD Triple Divergence, Weinstein Stage 2 rules, Mansfield RS, Auto-AVWAP, and Yahoo/Polygon/EDGAR API calls are public formulas on public market feeds. Any competent trading-platform user or Pine Script creator can replicate the core scans inside TradingView or TrendSpider in a weekend; a copycat could clone the UI in month six.
2. **No network effects and weak brand/IP clarity**: One trader using the scanner does not make it better for the next trader, and the product currently carries "Trullàs" alongside third-party names (`TrendSpiderSuite`, `LuxAlgoSuite`, `TradingWarz`) embedded in module nomenclature, risking IP confusion or cease-and-desist exposure before any brand equity forms.
3. **Undefined, zero-cost distribution engine**: Retail trading software is saturated with incumbents who dominate SEO, broker partnerships, and creator affiliates. Relying on generic "YouTube/X breakdowns" without a clear distribution wedge or captive community makes Customer Acquisition Cost likely higher than early LTV (open question for `founder-marketing` and `founder-cfo`).

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the small, specific beachhead market where Trullàs 24/7 can own >50% of active buyers rather than competing head-on with TradingView's 50M+ users and TrendSpider's multi-year brand? (Answers needed from `founder-competitors` and `founder-marketing`.)
- What is the "secret" here that incumbents structurally refuse to build — for example, proving whether filters and rules actually add net alpha via an immutable public shadow-signal audit trail?
- How will you protect against commercial data-redistribution lockouts and legal friction over third-party module names (`TrendSpiderSuite`, `LuxAlgoSuite`, `Trullàs`)? (Answers needed from `founder-brand` and `founder-ops`.)
