# Offers lens
Score: 6 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **High-urgency market with money to spend**: Self-directed swing traders with $10,000–$250,000 accounts regularly lose thousands on undisciplined entries, choppy market regimes, and sizing mistakes, and already spend $30–$150/month on charts, scanners, and trade journals.
- **High-value workflow consolidation**: Combining Weinstein Stage filtering, MACD Triple Divergence scanning, regime sizing, SEC EDGAR Form 4 context, and shadow R-multiple tracking solves multiple pain points under one roof instead of forcing traders to stitch three subscriptions together.
- **Low marginal software delivery cost once data is licensed**: Onboarding checklists, playbooks, alert templates, and deterministic backtest/shadow stats cost near zero per extra subscriber.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Unproven likelihood of outcome ("does it actually have an edge?")**: The brief admits `Spike R` and `Spike E` (whether the Regime Score discriminates and whether MACD Triple Divergence / Weinstein setups beat random entry net of costs over 5 years) have not been verified yet. Selling a trading scanner on unverified strategy performance collapses the Value Equation's likelihood score and spikes Month-2 churn.
2. **Data and runtime credibility gap**: Using unofficial Yahoo Finance data while branding the product "24/7" when the server isn't actually running 24/7 (`is247Active: false`) destroys buyer trust the first time a trader checks `/api/system-health` or misses an intraday trigger.
3. **$49/month sits in an awkward commodity middle**: At $49/month without a stacked guarantee, fast first-win onboarding, or verified historical expectancy metrics, buyers will compare it feature-for-feature against TradingView or Finviz Elite on price alone rather than outcome.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What specific measurable outcome does a subscriber get in their first 7 days (e.g., "zero chasing extended stocks and a curated 15-name Stage-2 watchlist before market open")? (Answers needed from `founder-consumer` and `founder-offer`.)
- What does Spike E actually show over 5 years net of slippage — positive expectancy over random entry, or market drift?
- What is the per-subscriber cost of a legal commercial market-data API (Polygon/Databento/Nasdaq) and cloud worker hosting, and can $49/month carry at least an 80% gross margin plus a clear onboarding guarantee? (Answers needed from `founder-ops` and `founder-cfo`.)
- Who specifically converts fastest — options income traders, US equities swing traders, or systematic Python/API tinkerers? (Answers needed from `founder-consumer`.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Complete and publish honest backtest/shadow evidence (`Spike E` and `Spike R`) showing signal behavior net of realistic slippage and survivorship caveats before making any performance-oriented marketing claims.
2. Replace unofficial Yahoo Finance feeds with a commercially licensed data API and move scans to a true external scheduled worker before taking recurring subscription money.
3. Re-frame the $49/month price around a concrete outcome stack (curated daily Stage-2 + divergence shortlist before the bell, fail-closed risk guardrails, automated journal + 30-day transparent shadow ledger) rather than selling "another chart scanner."
4. Validate on the consumer panel that target swing traders will actually pay above competitor medians (check via `founder-competitors` and `founder-pricing`).
