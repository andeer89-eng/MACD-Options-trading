## What would flip a no

- Show a 5-year net-of-cost backtest beating random entries and let me test a 14-day trial without a card on file.
- Publish a live, unedited 90-day shadow signal ledger upfront so I can audit the wins and losses before signing up.
- Price an EOD-only digest under $20/mo that replaces my trade-journal spreadsheet automatically.
- Include an open methodology and historical R-multiple report net of 5-10 bps slippage before checkout.
- Bundle the scanner, journal, and regime filter around $29/mo with a 30-day money-back guarantee so I can drop another tool.
- Show verified shadow signal stats and offer a $29/mo swing tier with a free 14-day trial.
- Drop an EOD daily watchlist tier to $25-$29/mo or clearly include real-time exchange data at $49.
- Open a read-only preview of the past 60 days of shadow signals and their net R outcomes.
- Show verified licensed data with zero delay and let the built-in journal replace my spreadsheet at $35-$39/mo.
- Give a 14-day risk-free trial or $29/mo nightly prep tier after I verify sample daily watchlists.
- Clearly separate licensed real-time feeds from delayed tiers and share verifiable third-party user reviews plus 90-day shadow results.
- Offer an EOD-focused prep tier around $25-$29/mo so I do not pay for intraday features I cannot use at work.
